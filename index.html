01 / The challenge

The biggest waste
is the waste we don't see.

We focus on three practical sources of avoidable energy waste in hotels. Select each challenge in the AI Studio to calculate an illustrative waste estimate from baseline and observed consumption.

THE PROBLEM / R1

High energy consumption isn't just a meter reading.

It is a planning problem. The opportunity is to connect demand signals to operational choices before unnecessary consumption becomes routine.

ENERGY × OCCUPANCY × TIMING
01

HVAC & air conditioning

Cooling or heating running longer than needed in shared or low-use areas.

02

Lighting in low-use spaces

Lights staying on in meeting rooms, corridors or facilities when demand is low.

03

Laundry & hot water

Equipment cycles and hot-water demand that may be poorly aligned with occupancy.

05 / Intended impact

Better decisions.
Measurable outcomes.

The aim is not to produce an impressive AI score. It is to make an operational decision clearer, easier to review and possible to measure.

OUTCOME / 01↘

Reduce avoidable energy use

Spot schedule and operating patterns that may contribute to unnecessary consumption.

OUTCOME / 02⌘

Give teams useful context

Bring key signals into one dashboard and turn patterns into practical recommendations.

OUTCOME / 03✳

Keep comfort in the loop

Use staff-reviewed actions and measure efficiency without treating guest comfort as optional.
